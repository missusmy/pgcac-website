Sponsorship levels for 2025 (in USD)

Anybody can donate to PGCA to support the PostgreSQL project, not just corporations. But if you have commercial interests in supporting the PostgreSQL project and you want to make a donation to help the project protect and defend our trademarks, these sponsorship levels are used.

Level Minimum Donation in USD (from Jan 2025)
Donor < $2,500
Friend $2,500
Supporter $5,000
