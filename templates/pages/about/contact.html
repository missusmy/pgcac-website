Contact the PGCA Board

PostgreSQL Community Association (PGCA)

The PostgreSQL Community Association is a Canadian Not For Profit corporation that holds assets for the PostgreSQL project such as domain names and trademarks.

Email is preferred

You can contact the board of the PostgreSQL Community Association at
board@postgres.ca
