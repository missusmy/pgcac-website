About

The PostgreSQL Community Association is a Canadian Not For Profit corporation that holds assets for the PostgreSQL project such as domain names and trademarks.

PostgreSQL Community Association is a trade or business name of the PostgreSQL Community Association of Canada. You may see the longer name in legal documents, asset registrations, and contracts etc.
