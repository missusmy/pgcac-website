Sponsors of PGCA

PostgreSQL Community Association (PGCA)

The PGCA cannot carry out its mission to protect the PostgreSQL project’s trademarks & brand assets without the generous financial support of our donors and sponsors from corporations, small businesses, & individual donors.

Every donation makes a difference. We especially thank our sponsors listed here for their sizable contributions at the Friends level and above.

Sponsorship levels for 2025 (in USD)

Anybody can donate to PGCA to support the PostgreSQL project, not just corporations. But if you have commercial interests in supporting the PostgreSQL project and you want to make a donation to help the project protect and defend our trademarks, these sponsorship levels are used.

(Amounts in USD. Sponsorship amounts will be going up as of Feb 1, 2026.)

Level Minimum Donation in USD (from Jan 2025)
Donor < $2,500
Friend $2,500
Supporter $5,000
Patron $15,000
Benefactor $30,000

PGCA Board and PostgreSQL Core Team

“Help us protect and steward the PostgreSQL open source project. Your financial sponsorship is an important (and necessary) part of the equation.”

To learn more about how you can help, review the PGCA sponsorship prospectus or contact us at 💌 donations@postgres.ca.
