Where will your sponsorship dollars go?

Donating to PGCA strengthens the business of Postgres and is needed because the funding for the volunteer-run PGCA non-profit comes from donations from corporations & individuals (like you!)

Your funding supports:

  • Ability to defend against inappropriate use of brand assets & trademarks
  • Ability to provide no-cost licenses to organizations who wish to use PostgreSQL brand assets outside of fair use
  • Ability to maintain domains required by the project, e.g. postgresql.org
  • Expansion of jurisdiction of trademark protection (Madrid Protocol)

How PGCA recognizes your donations?

For all donations at the “Friend” level & above—for the 12 months following your donation to PGCA

  1. Prominent listing of your name or logo on the PostgreSQL website at postgresql.org/about/financial
  2. You may state you are an official sponsor of the PostgreSQL project
