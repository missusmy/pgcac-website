Board members confirmed

31 May 2018

Following an AGM and board meeting at PGCon 2018 in Ottawa, the PostgreSQL Community Association of Canada's board now consists of:

  • Dave Page (Chairman)
  • Chris Browne (Treasurer)
  • Steve Singer (Secretary)
  • Jaime Casanova
  • Peter Eisentraut
  • Marc Fournier
  • Jonathan Katz
