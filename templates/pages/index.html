Change of Name

3 July 2023

Following discussion with the PostgreSQL Core Team, the Association has now registered PostgreSQL Community Association (PGCA) as a business name and will primarily use that name in the future.

The PostgreSQL Community Association of Canada remains the legal name of the Association, and may still be seen in official documents, contracts, and elsewhere.

Infringing Trademark Registrations Invalidated by Spanish Court

23 June 2022

In 2020, the PostgreSQL Core Team was made aware of trademarks registered in Spain and the European Union for POSTGRESQL and POSTGRESQL COMMUNITY by Fundación PostgreSQL, a 3rd-party nonprofit organization. Subsequently, Fundación PostgreSQL also filed trademark registrations for POSTGRES. This situation is described in two separate announcements by the PostgreSQL community: "Trademark Actions Against the PostgreSQL Community" and "Update on the Trademark Actions Against the PostgreSQL Community".

Fundación PostgreSQL offered publicly and privately to transfer the trademark registrations to PGCA at no cost. However, when prompted to initiate the transfer, Fundación PostgreSQL refused. This forced both PGCA and PostgreSQL Europe (PGEU), a recognized PostgreSQL nonprofit organization that operates in Europe, to go forward with court proceedings in Spain to invalidate the trademark registrations.

On the 31st of May, the Spanish court ruled in favor of PGCA and PGEU and invalidated the POSTGRESQL and POSTGRESQL COMMUNITY trademarks that were registered by Fundación PostgreSQL.

Legal action on the registration for POSTGRES is ongoing.

Fundación PostgreSQL attempted to register the same three trademarks in the United States. These applications were rejected by the examiner and are now abandoned.

The PGCA board is grateful for the support that the PostgreSQL community has provided for the defense of our trademarks. As we have stated previously, we do hope for an amicable resolution in this matter, but will pursue all options until Fundación PostgreSQL surrenders all claims to trademarks that are in violation of the PostgreSQL trademark policy.

If you would like to continue to help support the mission of PGCA of holding community assets such as tradearks and domain names, we kindly ask that you please consider donating.
